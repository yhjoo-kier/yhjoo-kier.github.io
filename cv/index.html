Research areas

  • Codesign of energy systems — equipment geometry, supervisory control, and the physics-based digital twin that links them, treated as a single coupled problem.
  • Physics-based digital twins of industrial plants — Simulink and Modelica plant models, CFD-derived reduced-order models, and multiscale process couplings, calibrated against field measurements.
  • AI agents for industrial energy operation — LLM-based supervisory control over an ontology / TCP·UDP / PLC stack, designed and validated on the digital twin before deployment.
  • Energy efficiency of manufacturing processes — battery electrode drying, industrial HVAC, steel casting.
  • Topology optimization of thermal systems — heat sinks, heat exchangers, thermal insulators under multi-physics constraints.
  • Machine learning for engineering simulation — deep-learned finite elements, graph-based convergence acceleration, deep reinforcement learning.
